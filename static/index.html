    Shelve an alarm (maintenance)

    Shelving stops the emails and text messages for one alarm for a set time, for example while a pump is being repaired. The alarm is still recorded below and the shelf ends by itself after the hours chosen (up to 72). Every shelve and unshelve is in the Audit log.

A PLC alarm stays on the list until it is acknowledged, even after it clears; the sludge tank FULL and EQ tank FULL alarms acknowledge themselves once they clear. Acknowledgements are audited.

    Excel report

    Up to 92 inclusive calendar days (one quarter), America/Vancouver days. Each tag worksheet lists Timestamp UTC, Timestamp America/Vancouver, Raw Value and Unit Of Measure. Chemical usage gives the litres of coagulant and flocculant used per day, month and quarter (from the Coag_Pump_Run and Floc_Pump_Run ml/min readings); the All report includes it as a Chemical Usage worksheet. Download CSV gives the same readings for the chosen tag (or all tags) as a CSV file. The Summary worksheet shows Total_Flow_Li in litres and cubic metres (m³ = L ÷ 1000) with the average EQ pH: each day's EOD value (the last reading above 1 L that day), the daily usage (EOD minus the previous EOD), notes for carried-forward days and meter drops, and monthly and quarterly totals, to 2 decimal places.

    Automatic email reports

    Report recipients, days and times are under Settings. Daily and monthly emails contain the previous day/month sample count and two-decimal minimum, average and maximum values, and the list of alarms in that period (time, alarm, still active or cleared, and who acknowledged it). The monthly email attaches the Excel report for the month (Summary and Total_Flow_Li readings); the quarterly email attaches the Excel Summary for the quarter. Sending requires configured SMTP credentials and the systemd scheduler.

    Help — how to use the Wastewater Dashboard

    This dashboard shows the wastewater treatment system live from the PLC (through the Ewon and Talk2M), keeps the history, raises alarms, emails reports and keeps the operating logs (sludge pickups, chemicals, sensor calibrations). All times are Vancouver time. The page refreshes by itself every 10 seconds.

    Each tab has its own colour, and its page uses the same colour on the left edge of each panel, so you can always see which page you are on: Overview Trends Alarms Audit Reports Settings Help

    Quick start for operators

    1. Start of shift: open Overview. The bar at the top should be green (LIVE API-BACKED READINGS) and there should be no red alarm banner.
    2. If there is a red banner or "Active alarms" box: go to Alarms, check the equipment, then press Acknowledge on the alarm.
    3. When the sludge truck has emptied the tank (every Monday and Thursday): Reports → Sludge pickup log → Log pickup.
    4. When chemicals arrive: Reports → Chemical purchases → Mark received (or log the purchase with its received date).
    5. Start of each month, and when a drum or pail is changed: Reports → Chemical stock → Log tank count for each chemical.
    6. After calibrating the pH or EQ tank level sensor: Audit → Sensor calibration log (Engineering or Admin).

    Overview

    Status bar (top of every page):

    • Green all four readings are live; shows the pH limits.
    • Red pH is outside its limits, or NO NEW DATA — nothing has arrived for longer than the data-stopped limit (check the Ewon, Talk2M and the server).
    • Amber one or more readings are missing, invalid or older than 3 minutes.

    Red alarm banner (flashing): one line for each PLC alarm that is ON right now, with the time it started and its PLC address — Sludge tank FULL, EQ tank FULL, E-Stop, Mixer pump, Rake motor, MAX RGT pump and Floc / Coag pump faults. It disappears by itself when the PLC bit goes back to 0. A grey dashed note under it lists any shelved alarms.

    Reading cards: EQ pH, EQ tank level (ft), discharge flow and total flow (litres). LIVE means the reading is current; STALE, INVALID or MISSING means it is not. Click the flow unit to switch between L/min and gal/min (remembered on this computer). Hover a card to see the exact time of the reading.

    Active alarms box: alarms still open or not yet acknowledged. Red when something is not acknowledged. Click it to open the list; Open Alarms page takes you to the full list.

    Process diagram (Show process diagram):

    • SYSTEM ENABLED circle: green ON / red OFF from PLC B3:0/4. Grey "--" means no reading has arrived.
    • EQ tank pH and level, rake motor speed (Hz), discharge flow, coagulant and flocculant pump rate (%) and run rate (choose ml/min or ml/h).
    • Electric transfer (sump) pump and circulation (mixer) pump: spinning green fan and RUNNING, or OFF.
    • Sludge tank: NORMAL or flashing FULL.
    • Pickup box: PICKUP EVERY MON & THU and the last pickup logged.

    The caustic and acid dosing pumps and other equipment drawings are illustrative only; they are not connected to the PLC.

    Trends

    Separate history charts for EQ pH, EQ tank level, discharge flow and total flow.

    • Window: last 6 hours, 24 hours or 7 days. The charts refresh every 30 seconds; Refresh updates them now.
    • Grid lines every 30 minutes (6-hour and 24-hour windows) or every 6 hours (7 days); the brighter line is midnight.
    • A gap in a line means no readings for more than 3 minutes (communication loss), not a zero reading.
    • The flow unit follows the L/min / gal/min choice on the Overview.

    For longer periods or exact numbers, use Reports.

    To acknowledge: check the equipment first, then press Acknowledge. Your name and the time are saved.

    Shelve an alarm (maintenance): stops the emails and texts for one alarm for 1–72 hours, with a reason — for example while a pump is being repaired. The alarm is still recorded, the shelf ends by itself, and every shelve is in the Audit log. Needs the Settings right. Unshelve ends it early.

    These are software alarms for monitoring. They do not replace the PLC safety interlocks.

    Reports

    Sludge pickup log: after the truck has emptied the sludge tank, enter the date and time and an optional note (hauler, truck, volume) and press Log pickup. Download monthly report shows each pickup and how long after the sludge tank FULL alarm it happened.

    Chemical purchases: log each purchase — product (with part #), purchase date, quantity (drums / pails, litres or US gallons), total cost (optional), PO / invoice number and supplier.

    • Fill in Received if it has arrived; otherwise leave it blank and press Mark received when it comes. Only received purchases add to stock.
    • Download purchases report (choose the year): totals per month, quarter and year, all years, and the full list with days to receive. A blank cost is estimated from the price per container in Settings and marked "estimated".

    Chemical stock: a card for each product shows the estimated stock in litres and drums / pails.

    • Stock = last tank count + purchases received since − usage. Coagulant and flocculant usage comes from their dosing pumps; caustic (NEUTRO 5045) and SA-93 have no pump reading yet, so their usage shows only when a new tank count is logged.
    • Log tank count regularly: the start of each month and whenever a drum or pail is changed.
    • Download consumption report (choose the year): delivered and used per month and year for each product, with costs once prices are entered.

    Excel report (choose the type and dates, up to 92 days; or CSV):

    • EQ_Tank_Level_Ft, FLOW_RATE, Total_Flow_Li, Rake_Speed_VFD — every reading, plus a Summary of daily, monthly and quarterly total flow in litres and cubic metres (m³) (EOD value and usage), with the pH average for each day, month and quarter.
    • Chemical usage — litres of coagulant and flocculant used per day, month and quarter (from the dosing pumps).
    • Sludge pickups & tank FULL alarms.
    • Alarm history — every alarm: start, clear, minutes active, who acknowledged it and how fast.
    • Daily discharge compliance — pH minimum / average / maximum, minutes outside the pH limits, litres discharged, peak flow and Compliant yes / NO for each day.
    • Pump run hours and starts — transfer (sump) and circulation (mixer) pumps.
    • Chemical purchases and chemical consumption — for the year of the From date.
    • Sensor calibrations — all calibrations of the pH and EQ tank level sensors.
    • All — one worksheet per tag.

    Audit

    Sensor calibration log (pH sensor and EQ tank height / level sensor):

    • After each calibration enter the date, who calibrated it, the certificate number and / or the certificate file (PDF, JPG or PNG up to 700 KB), and a note (buffers, reference values). Click a file name to download the certificate.
    • The cards show the last calibration and when the next is due: Next due, Due in … days or OVERDUE. A reminder email is sent before it is due and when it is overdue.
    • Download calibration report (Excel or CSV) for inspections.

    Audit history: who did what and when — sign-ins locked, settings changed, alarms acknowledged or shelved, pickups, purchases, tank counts and calibrations logged or removed, and every email and text sent or failed. Entries cannot be edited.

    Settings (Admin and Engineering)

    • pH alarm limits and how many readings in a row confirm an alarm and its recovery.
    • Alarm notification schedule: days and hours when alarm emails and texts are sent.
    • Data, tank level, flow and PLC alarms: turn each alarm on or off and set its limits.
    • Calibration reminders and chemical stock: how often each sensor must be calibrated, reminder days, low-stock levels and the price per container of each chemical.
    • Text message alerts and Email recipients and reports (daily, monthly and quarterly).
    • Accounts and passwords: add or remove logins and choose the rights of each role (Admin only).

    Press Save settings after changing anything; changes are in the Audit log.

    Emails and text messages

    • Alarm email: one email when an alarm comes on, and one when it clears (if the first was sent). Sent only within the alarm notification schedule and not for shelved alarms.
    • Text message: a short text for each new alarm to the phone addresses in Settings (if turned on).
    • Daily report: the previous day's readings, a discharge compliance line and the list of alarms.
    • Monthly and quarterly reports with an Excel attachment.
    • Calibration reminders at the daily report time.

    Troubleshooting

    • NO NEW DATA / readings STALE: check the Ewon has power and internet, and that Talk2M shows it online. Readings catch up by themselves once the connection is back — nothing is lost.
    • A PLC status shows "--" (for example SYSTEM ENABLED): no reading has arrived for that tag. It needs historical logging (with a logging interval) on the Ewon.
    • Page looks old after an update: press Ctrl+F5. The revision is shown top right.
    • A tab or button is missing: your login does not have that right — ask the Admin.
    • Logged something by mistake: use Remove on that row and log it again (both are in the Audit log).

    Settings and notification scheduling

    pH alarm limits and confirmation

    Each reading timestamp counts once. Incident emails are unique per confirmed event, not limited to once per day. This software alarm is not a substitute for a safety or compliance interlock.

    Alarm notification schedule

    Vancouver local time; overnight schedules supported.

    Data, tank level, flow and PLC alarms

    The high-level alarm uses the same consecutive-reading confirmation as pH. Emails for these alarms follow the alarm notification schedule and need Enable alarm email above; they do not need pH alarm monitoring turned on.

    Calibration reminders and chemical stock

    Chemical prices (per container, leave blank if not known yet)

    Reminders are sent at the daily report time. Low stock alarms follow the alarm notification schedule. Prices are used for the cost columns of the chemical consumption report.

    Text message alerts

    Texts are sent by email to your mobile carrier's email-to-text address (your phone number @ the carrier's SMS gateway; ask your carrier for it). They use the same email server and alarm schedule, and are not sent for shelved alarms. Send yourself a test before relying on it: some carriers delay or block these messages.
