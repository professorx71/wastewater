    Automatic email reports

    Report recipients, days and times are under Settings. Daily and monthly emails contain the previous day/month sample count and two-decimal minimum, average and maximum values, the chemical cost per m³ discharged, and the list of alarms in that period (time, alarm, still active or cleared, and who acknowledged it). The monthly email attaches the Excel report for the month (Summary and Total_Flow_Li readings); the quarterly email attaches the Excel Summary for the quarter. Sending requires configured SMTP credentials and the systemd scheduler.

    Help — how to use EffluentIQ

    EffluentIQ — Industrial Wastewater Monitoring & Reporting — shows the wastewater treatment system live from the PLC (through the Ewon and Talk2M), keeps the history, raises alarms, emails reports and keeps the operating logs (sludge pickups, chemicals, sensor calibrations). All times are Vancouver time. The page refreshes by itself every 2 seconds. The revision running now is shown top right and at the bottom of every page.

    Each tab has its own colour, and its page uses the same colour on the left edge of each panel, so you can always see which page you are on: Overview Trends Alarms Audit Reports Settings Help

    Quick start for operators

    1. Start of shift: open Overview. The bar at the top should be green (LIVE READING with the current date and time) and there should be no red alarm banner.
    2. If there is a red banner or "Active alarms" box: go to Alarms and check the equipment. The counter next to each alarm shows how long it has been on. Alarms are acknowledged automatically; there is no button to press.
    3. When the sludge truck has emptied the tank (every Monday and Thursday): Reports → Sludge pickup log → Log pickup.
    4. When chemicals arrive: Reports → Chemical purchases → Mark received (or log the purchase with its received date).
    5. Start of each month, and when a drum or pail is changed: Reports → Chemical stock → Log tank count for each chemical.
    6. After calibrating the pH or EQ tank level sensor: Audit → Sensor calibration log (Engineering or Admin).

    Overview

    Status bar (top of every page):

    • Green LIVE READING with the current date and time (Vancouver): all four readings are live.
    • Red pH is outside its limits, or NO NEW DATA — nothing has arrived for longer than the data-stopped limit (check the Ewon, Talk2M and the server).
    • Amber one or more readings are missing, invalid or older than 3 minutes.

    Live readings: when live readings are set up, the cards, the process diagram and the alarm checks use the value read straight from the Ewon (Talk2M M2Web), normally every 5 seconds, so "Updated" stays within seconds. A live value older than 1 minute is not used: the card falls back to the last logged reading. Trends, reports, chemical usage and costs always use the logged history (Talk2M DataMailbox), which arrives in batches from the Ewon. Hover over a card to see which one it is showing.

    AI prediction on the reading cards: next to each live reading, the arrow and % show where it is heading over the next hour, and the grey number under it is the predicted reading in 60 minutes. ↑ green = rising, ↓ red = falling, → grey = steady (less than 0.05%). The prediction carries on the trend of the last 30 minutes, so it is a guide, not a guarantee: a sudden change (a new batch, a pump starting) shows up within a few minutes. Hover over it for the rate per hour. It is not shown while a reading is not live.

    Red alarm banner (flashing): one line for each PLC alarm that is ON right now, with the time it started and its PLC address — Sludge tank FULL, EQ tank FULL, E-Stop, Mixer pump, Rake motor, MAX RGT pump and Floc / Coag pump faults. It disappears by itself when the PLC bit goes back to 0. A grey dashed note under it lists any shelved alarms.

    Reading cards: EQ pH, EQ tank level (ft), discharge flow and total flow (litres). LIVE means the reading is current; STALE, INVALID or MISSING means it is not. Click the flow unit to switch between L/min and GPM (remembered on this computer). Hover a card to see the exact time of the reading.

    Active alarms box: alarms whose condition is still present, each with a live active 12 min counter showing how long it has been on. Red while any alarm is active. Click it to open the list; Open Alarms page takes you to the full list.

    Process diagram (Show process diagram):

    • SYSTEM ENABLED circle: green ON / red OFF from PLC B3:0/4. Grey "--" means no reading has arrived.
    • EQ tank pH and level, rake motor speed (Hz), discharge flow, coagulant, flocculant, caustic (blue box) and acid (yellow box) pump rate (%) and run rate (choose mL/min or L/hr; one choice for all four pumps).
    • Coagulant, flocculant, caustic and acid pumps: RUNNING while the run reading is at least 0.01 mL/min, STOPPED below that (a leftover reading such as 0.003 mL/min shows as 0.00 and counts as stopped). Point at the word to see the exact reading from the PLC.
    • EQ tank water level: the water in the EQ tank drawing rises and falls with the live level reading, from 0 ft at the bottom to 6 ft at the top plate, with marks at each foot. Hover over the tank to see the level and % full.
    • Electric transfer (sump) pump and circulation (mixer) pump: spinning green fan and RUNNING, or OFF. The transfer pump fan turns clockwise, the circulation pump fan counter-clockwise.
    • Sludge tank: NORMAL or flashing FULL.
    • DAF bottom drain: the orange pipe from the bottom of the DAF unit through the gate valve into the sludge bin. It is drawn for reference only; the valve position is not read from the PLC.
    • Pickup box: PICKUP EVERY MON & THU and the last pickup logged.

    The caustic and acid dosing pumps and other equipment drawings are illustrative only; they are not connected to the PLC.

    Trends

    Separate history charts for EQ pH, EQ tank level, discharge flow and total flow, then one graph for the four chemical dosing pumps.

    • Window: last 6 hours, 24 hours or 7 days. The charts refresh every 30 seconds; Refresh updates them now.
    • Grid lines every 30 minutes (6-hour and 24-hour windows) or every 6 hours (7 days); the brighter line is midnight.
    • A gap in a line means no readings for more than 3 minutes (communication loss), not a zero reading.
    • The flow unit follows the L/min / GPM choice on the Overview.
    • Chemical dosing pumps (below the four charts): the coagulant (teal), flocculant (orange), caustic (blue) and acid (yellow) run rates on one graph, in mL/min or L/hr. Tick or untick a pump to show or hide its line; Hide graph folds the whole graph away and Show graph brings it back. Both choices are remembered on this computer. The Ewon logs these on change, so each reading is held until the next one (a flat line is a steady rate).

    For longer periods or exact numbers, use Reports.

    Chemical purchases: log each purchase — product (with part #), purchase date, quantity (drums / pails, litres, US gallons, kg or lb), total cost (optional) in $ CAD or $ USD, PO / invoice number and supplier.

    • Fill in Received if it has arrived; otherwise leave it blank and press Mark received when it comes. Only received purchases add to stock.
    • kg and lb are turned into litres with the product's weight per litre (Settings → Chemical products; for example NEUTRO 5045 is 1.525 kg/L: 700 lb per 55 gal drum). The list shows the litres, the weight and the quantity as it was entered (for example "entered as 500 kg").

    Chemical stock: a card for each product in use shows the estimated stock in litres and containers, about how many days are left and the date to order by. The card turns amber when fewer than the delivery time + 7 days are left, and red with ORDER NOW when the stock lasts no longer than the delivery time.

    • Stock = last tank count + purchases received since − usage. A product measured by a dosing pump (coagulant, flocculant, caustic, acid) is reduced live by the pump reading, and its days left use the last 7 days of pumping. Other products (not linked to a dosing pump) change only when a tank count is logged; their days left use the tank counts of the last 90 days (at least two, a day or more apart) and allow for the days since the last count.
    • Log tank count regularly: the start of each month and whenever a drum or pail is changed. The quantity can be in containers, litres, US gallons, kg or lb.
    • Cards show the stock in kg and lb too, for products with a weight per litre. Show above the list of tank counts chooses the period.
    • Download consumption report (choose the year): delivered and used per month and year for each product, with costs once prices are entered.

    Chemical cost per m³ or per litre: what the chemicals cost for each cubic metre (or litre — choose in the panel heading; 1 m³ = 1,000 L) discharged, for this month so far, last month and the last 30 days, with the litres and cost of each product. It needs the price per container under Settings → Chemical products. Each price starts on a date: usage before a product's first price date is not in the cost, and the card says so (for example "Caustic (NEUTRO 5045): price starts 2026-11-25, usage before then is not in the cost"). To include it, open Prices for that product and give the price an earlier start date. The caustic and acid dosing pumps count like the coagulant and flocculant pumps once a product is linked to each one (Settings → Chemical products → Dosing pump: Caustic dosing pump / Acid dosing pump, with the price per container); until then their litres are shown as "Caustic pump" / "Acid pump · no product linked" and are not in the cost. The daily and monthly emails include the same figure.

    • Chemical cost per m³ discharged — chemical cost, m³ discharged, cost per m³ and cost per litre for the date range, each quarter, month and day, with the litres of each product.     • Sludge pickups & tank FULL alarms.
    • Alarm history — every alarm: start, clear, minutes active, who acknowledged it and how fast.
    • Daily discharge compliance — pH minimum / average / maximum, minutes outside the pH limits, litres discharged, litres discharged while pH was outside the limits, peak flow and Compliant yes / NO for each day; the % of time and % of volume inside the limits; and a list of every pH excursion (start, end, minutes, highest / lowest pH, litres discharged during it). The daily email includes the same figures for yesterday.
    • Pump run hours and starts — transfer (sump) and circulation (mixer) pumps.
    • Chemical purchases and chemical consumption — for the year of the From date.
    • Sensor calibrations — all calibrations of the pH and EQ tank level sensors.
    • All — one worksheet per tag.

Entries cannot be edited.

    Settings (Admin and Engineering)

    • pH alarm limits and how many readings in a row confirm an alarm and its recovery.
    • Alarm notification schedule: the hours and days alarm emails and texts are sent, and how often the "still active" follow-up repeats while an alarm stays on.
    • Alarm notification schedule: days and hours when alarm emails and texts are sent.
    • Data, tank level, flow and PLC alarms: turn each alarm on or off and set its limits, including the desired discharge flow (GPM) and allowed swing for the unstable flow alarm.
    • Calibration reminders: how often each sensor must be calibrated and how many days before to remind.
    • Chemical products: the chemicals used at the plant. Press Add product for a new one; change a name, part #, supplier, container, litres per container, how it is measured (tank counts or a dosing pump), the low-stock level, the delivery time (days an order takes); then press Save settings. Prices opens the product's price history: when the price changes, press Add price, enter the date the new price starts and the price per container, and save. Each price applies from its date until the next one, so reports for earlier months keep the old price; every price change is recorded in the Audit log. Until a product has a price, its costs are left blank. A product with purchases or tank counts cannot be deleted: untick In use to hide it from the lists (its history stays in the reports). Each dosing pump can measure only one product. Weight per litre (kg/L), from the product's SDS, lets purchases and tank counts be entered in kg or lb. Currency of the prices: $ CAD or $ USD, used for the cost per m³ and estimated purchase costs.
    • Text message alerts and Email recipients and reports (daily, monthly and quarterly).
    • Accounts and passwords: add or remove logins and choose the rights of each role (Admin only).

    Emails and text messages

    • Alarm email: one email when an alarm comes on, and one when it clears (if the first was sent). Sent only within the alarm notification schedule and not for shelved alarms.
    • "Still active" follow-ups for every alarm: while an alarm stays on, another email and text says how long it has been active, counted from when the alarm started — for example "Wastewater pH HIGH alarm — still active 30 min" or "Emergency Stop Activated — still active 1 h". With the default of 30 minutes they come at 30 min and 1 h, then every hour (2 h, 3 h …) until the alarm clears. Set the interval, or 0 to turn follow-ups off, in Settings → Alarm notification schedule. Warnings (pH trending, pump not dosing) and low stock / ORDER NOW get one email only. The recovery email says how long the alarm lasted.
    • Text message: a short text for each new alarm and each "still active" follow-up to the phone addresses in Settings (if turned on), with the reading — for example "Wastewater pH LOW alarm — still active 30 min. pH now 5.42, low limit 5.80. At Nov 22 17:19." It is sent without a subject line, so the phone shows the alarm once instead of "Subj: …" followed by the same words.
    • Daily report: the previous day's readings, a discharge compliance line, the chemical cost per m³ (once prices are entered) and the list of alarms.
    • Monthly report (with the month's chemical cost per m³) and quarterly report, each with an Excel attachment.
    • Senders: the daily, monthly and quarterly reports come from EffluentIQ Report <reports@octaveworks.com>. Alarm and recovery emails, "still active" follow-ups, text messages, calibration reminders and low stock / ORDER NOW come from EffluentIQ Alerts <alarm@octaveworks.com>.
    • Calibration reminders at the daily report time.
    • Low stock and ORDER NOW emails for each chemical (Settings → Chemical products).

    Troubleshooting

    • NO NEW DATA / readings STALE: check the Ewon has power and internet, and that Talk2M shows it online. Readings catch up by themselves once the connection is back — nothing is lost.
    • A PLC status shows "--" (for example SYSTEM ENABLED): no reading has arrived for that tag. It needs historical logging (with a logging interval) on the Ewon.
    • Page looks old after an update: press Ctrl+F5. The revision is shown top right.
    • A tab or button is missing: your login does not have that right — ask the Admin.
    • Logged something by mistake: ask the Admin to use Remove on that row (it asks for the Admin password), then log it again (both are in the Audit log). Wrong passwords count towards the sign-in lock: after 10 wrong passwords in 15 minutes it locks for 15 minutes.

    Settings and notification scheduling

    pH alarm limits and confirmation

    Each reading timestamp counts once. Each confirmed pH alarm is emailed once, then a "still active" follow-up says how long it has been on (for example "still active 30 min"), as set under Alarm notification schedule, until pH is back in range. This software alarm is not a substitute for a safety or compliance interlock.

    Alarm notification schedule

    Vancouver local time; overnight schedules supported.

    While an alarm stays on, another email and text says how long it has been active, counted from when the alarm started: with 30, at 30 min and 1 h, then every hour (2 h, 3 h …). Not for warnings or low stock / order now.

    Data, tank level, flow and PLC alarms

    Alarm "Discharge Flow Reading GPM not Stable — Note: Check Mixer Pump or Check Flow meter sensor" when, while discharging, the flow is outside the desired flow ± the allowed % for more than half the readings in that time. Uses the live readings (every few seconds).

    The high-level alarm uses the same consecutive-reading confirmation as pH. Emails for these alarms follow the alarm notification schedule and need Enable alarm email above; they do not need pH alarm monitoring turned on.

    Calibration reminders

    Reminders are sent at the daily report time.

    Chemical products

    The chemicals used at the plant. They fill the Chemical lists under Reports (purchases and tank counts) and the stock, cost and consumption reports. Measured by: a dosing pump reading (Coag_Pump_Run, Floc_Pump_Rate, Caustic_Pump_Run or Acid_Pump_Run) gives the usage live; other products use the tank counts. Delivery time is how many days an order takes to arrive: Order now is raised when the stock left lasts no longer than that. Prices: each product keeps a price history — add a new price with the date it starts, and every cost uses the price valid on that day, so earlier months keep the old price (leave it empty until the price is known). A product that has purchases or tank counts cannot be deleted: untick In use to hide it from the lists; its history stays in the reports.
