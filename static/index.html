Excel report

Up to 92 inclusive calendar days (one quarter), America/Vancouver days. Each tag worksheet lists Timestamp UTC, Timestamp America/Vancouver, Raw Value and Unit Of Measure. The Summary worksheet shows Total_Flow_Li in litres: each day's EOD value (the last reading above 1 L that day), and monthly and quarterly totals of those EOD values, to 2 decimal places.

Automatic daily and monthly email reports

Report recipients, days and times are under Settings. Reports contain the previous day/month sample count and two-decimal minimum, average and maximum values. Sending requires configured SMTP credentials and the systemd scheduler.

Settings and notification scheduling

pH alarm limits and confirmation

Each reading timestamp counts once. Incident emails are unique per confirmed event, not limited to once per day. This software alarm is not a substitute for a safety or compliance interlock.
