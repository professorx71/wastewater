    Excel report

    Up to 92 inclusive calendar days (one quarter), America/Vancouver days. Each tag worksheet lists Timestamp UTC, Timestamp America/Vancouver, Raw Value and Unit Of Measure. Chemical usage gives the litres of coagulant and flocculant used per day, month and quarter (from the Coag_Pump_Run and Floc_Pump_Run ml/min readings); the All report includes it as a Chemical Usage worksheet. Download CSV gives the same readings for the chosen tag (or all tags) as a CSV file. The Summary worksheet shows Total_Flow_Li in litres: each day's EOD value (the last reading above 1 L that day), the daily usage (EOD minus the previous EOD), notes for carried-forward days and meter drops, and monthly and quarterly totals, to 2 decimal places.

    Automatic email reports

    Report recipients, days and times are under Settings. Daily and monthly emails contain the previous day/month sample count and two-decimal minimum, average and maximum values. The monthly email attaches the Excel report for the month (Summary and Total_Flow_Li readings); the quarterly email attaches the Excel Summary for the quarter. Sending requires configured SMTP credentials and the systemd scheduler.

    Settings and notification scheduling

    pH alarm limits and confirmation

    Each reading timestamp counts once. Incident emails are unique per confirmed event, not limited to once per day. This software alarm is not a substitute for a safety or compliance interlock.

    Alarm notification schedule

    Vancouver local time; overnight schedules supported.

    Data, tank level and flow alarms

    The high-level alarm uses the same consecutive-reading confirmation as pH. Emails for these alarms follow the alarm notification schedule and need Enable alarm email above; they do not need pH alarm monitoring turned on.
